# 5-Minute Demonstration Video Script
## SIH26097: AI Voice Assistant for Livelihood Mapping and Skilling (PM-AJAY)

---

### [0:00 - 0:45] Minute 1: The Problem & The Mission
- **Visual**: Opening on the PM-AJAY Livelihood Assistant home screen with multilingual toggle (Telugu, Hindi, English).
- **Voiceover**: *"In rural and semi-urban India, thousands of capable youth and women under PM-AJAY miss out on livelihood schemes because traditional forms and websites demand high literacy. Today, we demonstrate our AI Voice Assistant for Livelihood Mapping, bringing empathy, local demand matching, and omni-channel access to the last mile."*

---

### [0:45 - 2:00] Minute 2: Beneficiary Voice Interaction & Skill Discovery
- **Visual**: Click microphone button. Beneficiary speaks in Telugu: *"I have been doing basic sewing at home for 2 years and also help my family with dairy animals."*
- **Voiceover**: *"Watch as the assistant listens, speaks back in warm Telugu, and automatically extracts verified NSQF skills: Sewing Machine Operation, Garment Pattern Cutting, and Cattle Feed Nutrition. No typing, no complex forms."*
- **Visual**: Opportunities page opens displaying customized matches: **Self-Employed Tailor (NSQF Level 3)** with local market demand level 5 in Warangal, direct links to PM Vishwakarma toolkit subsidy, and nearest training centers.

---

### [2:00 - 3:15] Minute 3: Multi-Channel Accessibility (WhatsApp & IVR)
- **Visual**: Screen switches to WhatsApp Web interface showing live conversation with the Twilio bot, followed by an IVR interactive voice simulation.
- **Voiceover**: *"Whether a beneficiary accesses the service through a Panchayat Kiosk, an affordable smartphone on WhatsApp, or an entry-level feature phone via toll-free IVR, the same unified AI brain delivers real-time skilling guidance."*

---

### [3:15 - 4:15] Minute 4: District Officer Command Center & Dropout Analytics
- **Visual**: Log in as `officer.warangal@demo.gov.in`.
- **Voiceover**: *"Here is the District Livelihood Officer dashboard for Warangal. The officer views the live 5-stage funnel: 100 registered beneficiaries, skills identified, certified, placed, and early dropout alerts. The AI flags candidates with high mobility or economic risk, allowing officers to coordinate counselor interventions."*
- **Visual**: Click **Generate Livelihood Action Plan**. System calculates optimized budget allocation and placement projections across high-demand trades.

---

### [4:15 - 5:00] Minute 5: Privacy, Security & Scalability Wrap-Up
- **Visual**: Privacy dashboard demonstrating explicit consent logs, zero caste storage proof, data export, and account deletion.
- **Voiceover**: *"Built with enterprise-grade security, helmet headers, strict JSON schema validation, and district scoping. SIH26097 transforms PM-AJAY skilling from passive registration into active, empathetic, and accountable livelihood empowerment."*
