# SIH26097: Slide Presentation Outline
## AI Voice Assistant for Livelihood Mapping and Skilling (PM-AJAY, GIA Component)

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
* **Title**: AI-Powered Multilingual Voice Assistant for PM-AJAY Livelihood Mapping & Skilling
* **Problem Statement ID**: SIH26097
* **Vision**: Bridging the skilling-to-livelihood divide for low-literacy rural beneficiaries through conversational AI, local market demand mapping, and automated administrative governance.

### Slide 2: Problem Statement Breakdown vs. Implemented Solution
| PM-AJAY / GIA Challenge | Implemented Technical Feature |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Low Literacy & Vernacular Divide** | Voice-first conversational AI supporting Telugu, Hindi, and English with dialect-aware empathy via Sarvam AI & Gemini. |
| **Last-Mile Access Barrier** | 4-in-1 Omni-Channel Accessibility: Web browser, Village Panchayat Kiosk, WhatsApp Bot, and IVR Telephony. |
| **Unverified / Mismatched Skilling** | 100% NSQF-aligned mapping with verified QP codes, NCO-2015 codes, and Skill India Digital Hub integration. |
| **High Training Dropout Rates** | Proactive AI Risk Scoring Engine factoring mobility constraints, educational baseline, and time availability. |
| **Fragmented Department Action** | Officer Assisted Registration, Inter-Departmental Task Engine, and District Analytics Funnel. |
| **Privacy & Dignity Concerns** | Strict No-Caste Policy, Explicit Multilingual Consent, Data Export & Account Purge rights. |

### Slide 3: Beneficiary Journey Walkthrough
* **Step 1: Spoken Discovery**: Beneficiary talks naturally about their daily work, family background, or interests without filling complex forms.
* **Step 2: AI Skill Extraction**: Contextual NLP matches conversational inputs to verified NSQF skill masters.
* **Step 3: Pathway Matching**: Dual-track recommendations for Wage Employment or Self-Employment (with business plan & loan links).
* **Step 4: Nearby Fulfillment**: Direct routing to verified local training centers and certified counselors.

### Slide 4: Officer & Governance Cockpit
* **District-Scoped Access**: Officers monitor real-time training-to-placement funnels in their district (Warangal, Adilabad, Nalgonda).
* **Demand vs. Supply Balancing**: Visual comparison of local market demand vs. available candidate pool.
* **Automated Action Plan Generation**: One-click generation of district skilling plans with budget allocation and placement projections.

### Slide 5: Innovation, Impact & Scalability
* **Cost Efficiency**: ₹12.50 per beneficiary conversational onboarding.
* **Scalability**: Cloud-native Dockerized Express/MongoDB architecture with Atlas clustering support.
* **Responsible AI**: Schema validation, prompt isolation, and zero demographic bias.
